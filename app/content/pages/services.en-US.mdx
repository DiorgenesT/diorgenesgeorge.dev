## What I do

**Indicator dashboards from scattered sources.** Data that lives today in spreadsheets, legacy systems and public portals becomes a single screen, with history and comparison. Every number on display carries the origin it came from.

**Portals and sites with static rendering.** Pages that load instantly, survive traffic spikes without scaling a server, and do not go down when it matters most. Accessibility goes in as a requirement, not as a final adjustment.

**Integrations and ETL between systems that do not talk.** Pulling data from APIs, public portals or spreadsheets, normalizing what arrives dirty, and loading it into a model that can answer a business question.

## How it works

1. **A diagnostic conversation.** Understand the problem before proposing a solution. No cost.
2. **A proposal with fixed scope.** What is in, what is out, and what changes the price.
3. **Delivery in usable increments.** You see it working before the end, not only at the end.
4. **Handover with documentation.** The system is yours, with the decisions written down.

## What I do not do

Saying it saves us both time.

- **I do not take on legacy maintenance without access to the code.** Without reading what exists, any estimate is a guess.
- **I do not ship a dashboard with numbers that have no traceable origin.** If the data has no owner and no update cadence, the dashboard becomes fiction wearing the appearance of data — and I will not put my name on that.
